\begin{abstract}
We present an empirical study investigating the causal dynamics and optimization limits of post-training reasoning enhancements in parameter-efficient, hardware-constrained regimes (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU, 8GB VRAM). We systematically trace the progression of mathematical reasoning through five decoupled paradigms: (1) \textbf{Self-Distillation Policy Optimization (SDPO)} with symbolic verifiers; (2) \textbf{Continuous Soft Mixture-of-Adapters (Soft-MoA)} across disjoint mathematical domains; (3) \textbf{Hardware-Constrained Long-Context Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)}; (4) \textbf{Continuous Manifold Regulation via Prefill Pondering ODEs}; and (5) \textbf{Test-Time Neurosymbolic Verification and Tuned Lens (J-Lens) Geometric Stability Gating}.

First, to isolate causal learning from stochastic weight perturbation on pre-selected error distributions, we introduce a \textbf{Placebo Negative Control Ablation}: updating an identical low-rank adapter ($r=4$, $\alpha=8$) over an identical gradient budget (17 optimization steps, learning rate $3 \times 10^{-5}$, Gram matrix subspace orthogonality regularizer $\lambda_{\mathrm{ortho}} = 0.005$) on unrelated canonical mathematics examples. Under strict greedy decoding ($T=0.0$), the \textbf{Placebo Control scored 0 / 35 (0.0\%)} on strictly held-out failure questions, whereas \textbf{Pure SDPO achieved 4 / 35 (11.4\%) zero-shot recovery} (Fisher exact two-sided $p = 0.114$, one-sided $p = 0.057$, McNemar paired test $p = 0.125$). While providing an encouraging directional signal, we document a \textbf{Pareto capacity trade-off}: incorporating static factual anchors to prevent localized representation drift on the exceptional Lie algebra $F_4$ ($52 \to 28$) reduced held-out recovery yield from 4 / 35 (11.4\%) to 2 / 35 (5.71\%). Furthermore, multi-domain scaling across 170 evaluation cases revealed the \textbf{Subspace Interference Effect}: joint multi-adapter fine-tuning induces cross-subspace covariance ($A_1 A_2^T \neq 0$), eroding delicate frontier representations ($E_7 = 133 \to 70$). In contrast, an \textbf{Untrained Soft Blender} modulated by Layer-32 Gaussian Phase Routing achieves peak multi-domain accuracy (138 / 170, 81.18\%), matching discrete regex routing while eliminating brittle manual parsing rules.

To expand reasoning horizons on consumer hardware, we introduce a \textbf{Zero-Paging VRAM Architecture} that offloads token embeddings to host CPU RAM, quantizes the linear language model head to 4-bit NF4, and computes policy gradients via chunked micro-batches ($\text{chunk\_size}=32$). This reduces baseline model footprint from 7,298 MB to 3,907 MB, unlocking 3,616 MB of physical headroom and enabling 1536--2048 token autoregressive rollout and policy updates with \textbf{zero WDDM driver paging} on commodity 8GB GPUs. Exploiting this horizon, \textbf{Long-Context GRPO ($L=1536$, $G=4$)} advances the frozen SFT baseline on the strictly unseen \textbf{Clean 125 Held-Out MATH-500} subset from \textbf{99 / 125 (79.20\%)} to \textbf{100 / 125 (80.00\%)} under the legacy grader---and from \textbf{103 / 125 (82.40\%)} to \textbf{104 / 125 (83.20\%)} under normalized symbolic equivalence (+1 net problem on Q105, exact McNemar $p = 1.0000$, 0 regressions; early-surrender frequency across all 26 SFT-failed held-out items remained unchanged at 15 / 26 vs.\ 15 / 26, confirming Q105 as an isolated single-item flip rather than a systematic change in deliberation persistence). Finally, a unified-grader ablation across four test-time inference modules on the Clean 125 Held-Out set demonstrates that: (i) \textbf{Prefill Pondering ODE (Module 1)} preserves \textbf{100 / 125 (80.00\% legacy; 104 / 125, 83.20\% normalized symbolic)} as a neutral invariance operator; (ii) naive regex-based \textbf{CST Symbolic PRM Gating (Module 2, $G=1$)} suffers a \textbf{$-1$ problem regression} (\textbf{99 / 125 legacy; 103 / 125 normalized symbolic}) due to a false-positive fraction contradiction on Q055; (iii) \textbf{Tri-Modal Tuned Lens Geometric Stability Gating (Module 3, $G=1$)} eliminates the Q055 false positive, restoring \textbf{100 / 125 (80.00\% legacy; 104 / 125, 83.20\% normalized symbolic)} with zero regressions while uncovering the \textbf{Confident Hallucination Phenomenon} under greedy ($T=0$) decoding: inter-layer affine consensus (Layers 28 \& 30 $\to$ Layer 31) is slightly higher on failed derivations (\textbf{0.7867}, $n=21$) than on correct derivations (\textbf{0.7756}, $n=104$; overall $0.7775 \pm 0.0191$, Top-1 vocabulary agreement \textbf{124 / 125 = 99.20\%}); and (iv) \textbf{Targeted Best-of-$G$ ($G=4$) Multi-Branch Neurosymbolic PRM Rollout (Module 4)} triggers blindly on 15 / 150 structurally flawed trajectories to advance Clean 125 Held-Out accuracy to \textbf{102 / 125 (81.60\% legacy)} and \textbf{106 / 125 (84.80\% normalized symbolic; 129 / 150 = 86.00\% combined)} (+2 Held-Out gains over GRPO on Q120 and Q138, 0 regressions, exact McNemar $p = 0.5000$). Crucially, a compute-matched comparison against \textbf{Pure Majority Voting (\texttt{MV@4} and \texttt{MV@5})} on the exact same stored candidate pool originally revealed an \textbf{exact aggregate tie at 102 / 125 legacy (106 / 125 normalized symbolic, net difference 0, McNemar $p = 1.0000$)}: PRM rescued base-5 arithmetic (Q138: $224_5 \to 204_5$) where majority voting failed, whereas majority voting rescued Q124 ($\text{GT} = 8$) where legacy PRM suffered a false-positive penalty driven by the Tuned Lens stability term. Replacing the uninformative internal geometric stability term (which yields an inverse $\text{AUROC} = 0.3721$ under single-pass greedy decoding) with pure Semantic Agreement + Capped CST Symbolic Verification decisively breaks this parity, simultaneously capturing both Q120, Q124, and Q138 to reach \textbf{107 / 125 (85.60\%)} with zero regressions ($+4$ net over SFT, McNemar $p = 0.1250$). Furthermore, under a ternary selective prediction framework (Correct $=+1$, Abstain $=0$, Wrong $=-\lambda$), applying a Tier-1 Selective Abstention Gate on completely divergent candidate distributions ($1/4$ agreement) eliminates 5/5 hallucinations with zero false abstentions, boosting selective accuracy to \textbf{89.17\%} at 96.0\% coverage ($+0.7520$ utility at $\lambda=1$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Experimental Progression \& Decoupled Architecture}

The SODALS v2.1 research pipeline investigates reasoning optimization across nine systematically controlled stages on Qwen 3.5 9B Base (NF4 Quantized / FP16 Native):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Stage 1 (Balanced SFT):} 815 master examples $\to$ Baseline Adapter (\texttt{balanced\_r4}, 389/500 on full MATH-500).
    \item \textbf{Stage 2 (Error Mining):} 111 MATH-500 failures $\to$ Stratified split (Seed 42) into 76 training failures and 35 strictly held-out unseen failures.
    \item \textbf{Stage 3 (SDPO \& Placebo Control):} 17 SymPy-verified self-corrections trained across Pure SDPO LoRA (17 steps), Placebo Control LoRA (17 unrelated math items, 17 steps), and Anchored SDPO-A (17 self-corrections + 3 domain anchors, 20 steps).
    \item \textbf{Stage 4 (Multi-Domain MoA):} Domain 1 (MATH-500) + Domain 2 (Lie Theory \& Abstract Algebra) governed by Layer-32 Gaussian Phase Routing (Untrained Soft Blender: 138/170, 81.18\%).
    \item \textbf{Stage 5 (Zero-Paging VRAM Architecture):} CPU Embedding Offload + 4-bit NF4 LM Head + Chunked Policy Loss, reducing base VRAM from 7,298 MB to 3,907 MB (unlocking $L=1536\text{--}2048$ with 0 WDDM page faults).
    \item \textbf{Stage 6 (Long-Context GRPO):} Group Relative Policy Optimization ($L=1536$, $G=4$ rollouts, 25 training prompts). Clean Held-Out MATH-500 (125Q): 100 / 125 (80.00\% legacy) and 104 / 125 (83.20\% normalized symbolic) vs.\ SFT 99 / 125 (79.20\% legacy) and 103 / 125 (82.40\% normalized symbolic), 0 drops, +1 gain (Q105).
    \item \textbf{Stage 7 (Prefill Pondering ODE --- Module 1):} Explicit RK4 continuous manifold relaxation on prefill latent states, preserving 100 / 125 legacy (104 / 125 normalized symbolic) on Clean Held-Out with 0 regressions.
    \item \textbf{Stage 8 (Test-Time Neurosymbolic PRM \& Tuned Lens Gating --- Modules 2 \& 3):} Unified-grader evaluation isolating grader normalization (+4 formatting matches across all models) from gate intervention, showing that Module 3 (Tuned Lens + Tri-Modal PRM) eliminates Module 2's Q055 false-positive regression ($99 \to 100/125$ legacy; $103 \to 104/125$ normalized symbolic) and reveals high inter-layer stability on failed greedy chains ($0.7867$ vs.\ $0.7756$).
    \item \textbf{Stage 9 (Targeted Best-of-$G$ Neurosymbolic Rollout --- Module 4):} Multi-branch sampling ($G=4$) with Tri-Modal PRM selection over 15 blindly triggered flaw candidates, achieving 102 / 125 (81.60\% legacy) and 106 / 125 (84.80\% normalized symbolic) on Clean Held-Out (+2 net gains on Q120 and Q138, 0 regressions, overruling majority voting on Q138).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Forensic Autopsy \& Scientific Analyses}

\subsection{Causal Attribution via Negative Control (Placebo Ablation)}

A critical vulnerability in post-training evaluations is the \textbf{boundary perturbation fallacy}: when evaluating on questions pre-selected for initial failure, an evaluation score of 0 / 35 before fine-tuning is an artifact of selection rather than an empirical baseline. To determine whether an arbitrary 17-step weight perturbation would flip borderline answers purely by chance, we trained the Placebo Control model on 17 unrelated mathematics examples (\texttt{control\_17\_curriculum.jsonl}).

The Placebo Control model achieved \textbf{0 / 35 (0.0\%)} on the held-out failure distribution, whereas Pure SDPO achieved \textbf{4 / 35 (11.4\%)}. Under Fisher's exact test, the 4/35 vs.\ 0/35 difference corresponds to a two-sided $p = 0.114$ and one-sided $p = 0.057$ (McNemar paired $p \approx 0.125$). While the zero-flip outcome of the placebo control provides an encouraging empirical contrast against random boundary perturbation, the sample size ($N=35$) is statistically underpowered to definitively reject the null hypothesis at $\alpha = 0.05$. Expanding the failure cohort to $N \ge 100$ is strictly required to establish asymptotic causal attribution.

\subsection{The $F_4$ Anomaly and Pristine 24Q Frontier STEM Decomposition}

In initial 8-question evaluations, the Placebo model scored 7 / 8 (87.5\%), outperforming Pure SDPO (6 / 8, 75.0\%) on Question 1:
\[
\text{Problem: What is the dimension of the exceptional Lie algebra } F_4? \quad (\text{Ground Truth: } 52)
\]
Placebo predicted \texttt{52} (PASS), whereas Pure SDPO predicted \texttt{28} (FAIL). An audit of \texttt{control\_17\_curriculum.jsonl} revealed that Item 17 in the Placebo set primed the Cartan root-dimension formula ($\dim(\mathfrak{g}) = \mathrm{rank}(\mathfrak{g}) + |\Phi|$) on $\mathfrak{g}_2$, enabling exact computation of $\dim(F_4) = 4 + 48 = 52$. Conversely, Pure SDPO experienced localized representation drift, misidentifying $F_4$ with $D_4 = \mathfrak{so}(8)$ ($28$).

To eliminate small-sample noise ($n=8$), we evaluated all models on the \textbf{Pristine 24Q Frontier STEM Benchmark} (3 canonical textbook invariant questions across 8 domains: Lie Theory, Modular Forms, Symplectic Geometry, Arithmetic Geometry, Algebraic Topology, Representation Theory, Complex Analysis, and Characteristic Classes). Across all 24 questions, 20 are resolved identically across all four models, with $F_4$ serving as the sole differentiator between Baseline (21/24), Placebo (21/24), Pure SDPO (20/24), and Anchored SDPO-A (21/24).

\subsection{Capacity Cost of Anchor Regularization and Epistemic Scope}

Incorporating 3 static frontier anchors alongside the 17 self-corrections in SDPO-A restored $F_4 = 52$ (21/24), but reduced held-out repair yield from \textbf{4 / 35 (11.4\%)} (Q6, Q8, Q9, Q35) to \textbf{2 / 35 (5.71\%)} (Q9, Q35). In a hyper-parameter-efficient regime ($r=4$, 20 steps), allocating 15\% of the gradient trajectory to static anchors dilutes optimization pressure away from generative error repair. Furthermore, all models consistently failed Question 7 ($\dim \mathfrak{sp}(6) = 21$, predicting \texttt{12}), demonstrating that post-training anchor regularization operates via \textbf{template-specific formula recall} rather than generalized symbolic deduction.

\subsection{Multi-Adapter Cartridge Routing \& Continuous Soft LoRA Blending}

To surpass single-adapter capacity ceilings across Domain 1 (Competition MATH-500) and Domain 2 (Abstract Algebra \& Lie Theory), SODALS introduces a modular \textbf{Mixture-of-Adapters (MoA)} governed by a Layer-32 \textbf{Gaussian Phase Router (GPR)} ($\tau = 0.004913$, Fisher separation $= 15.30$):
\begin{equation}
\Delta W = (1 - \alpha) s_1 W_1 + \alpha s_2 W_2, \quad \alpha = \sigma(z_i / 0.03)
\end{equation}
On the 170-question multi-domain benchmark (150 MATH-500 + 20 Abstract Algebra), the \textbf{Untrained Soft Blender} achieved \textbf{138 / 170 (81.18\%)} (122/150 Domain 1, 16/20 Domain 2), outperforming joint fine-tuning variants (Phase 3 Scratch: 128/170; Phase 3 Calibrated: 135/170; Phase 13 Warm-Start: 137/170). Joint training induced cross-subspace perturbation ($A_1 A_2^T \neq 0$), erasing $E_7 = 133 \to 70$ and corrupting $\mathfrak{sp}(6) = 21 \to 1$.

\subsection{Hardware-Constrained Long-Context VRAM Architecture}

To unlock the 1536--2048 token window on an 8GB laptop GPU (NVIDIA RTX 4060, 8,188 MB physical VRAM) without WDDM driver paging, we engineered three memory optimizations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Host CPU Embedding Offload (\texttt{CPUEmbeddingWrapper}):} Transfers the 248,320-token lookup table to CPU RAM ($<0.1\text{ ms}$ transfer overhead), freeing \textbf{1,645 MB} VRAM.
    \item \textbf{4-Bit NF4 LM Head Quantization:} Quantizes the frozen $[248320, 4096]$ projection matrix from FP16 (1,940 MB) to NF4 (508 MB), saving \textbf{1,431 MB} VRAM.
    \item \textbf{Chunked Policy Cross-Entropy Loss ($\text{chunk\_size}=32$):} Caps intermediate vocabulary logit memory at \textbf{15.8 MB}.
\end{enumerate}
Base VRAM plummeted from \textbf{7,298 MB to 3,907 MB}, opening \textbf{3,616 MB} of physical headroom (Table~\ref{tab:vram}).

\subsection{Long-Context Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)}

With the zero-paging 1536-token window operational, we trained a policy via Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)~\cite{shao2024deepseekmath}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{GRPO}}(\theta) = -\frac{1}{G} \sum_{i=1}^G \left( \min \left( r_i(\theta) A_i, \mathrm{clip}(r_i(\theta), 1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon) A_i \right) - \beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi_\theta \| \pi_{\mathrm{ref}}) \right)
\end{equation}
across 25 competition prompts ($G=4$, 100 rollouts). To prevent train-test contamination, the 25 training prompts are strictly segregated (\texttt{Table~\ref{tab:grpo_train}}) from the 125 unseen held-out evaluation questions (\texttt{Table~\ref{tab:grpo_clean}}).

\paragraph{Statistical Disclosure \& Uninformative Policy-Movement Null (\texttt{EFFECT\_UNMEASURED\_POLICY\_DID\_NOT\_MOVE}):}
On the 125 unseen exploratory dev problems, the 25-prompt GRPO pilot differs from SFT by $+1$ isolated flip (Q105, exact two-sided McNemar $p = 1.0000$). Forensic weight and trajectory auditing (\texttt{FORENSIC\_KL\_AND\_EPISTEMIC\_AUDIT\_REPORT.json}) reveals that the relative Frobenius weight change was only $\|\Delta W\|_F / \|W_0\|_F = 0.2891\%$ (max layer $0.9258\%$), leaving \textbf{109 / 125} greedy trajectories token-length-identical and \textbf{118 / 125} final answers identical (mean $|\Delta\text{tok}| = 13.23$). Across all 26 SFT-failed held-out items, premature termination ($<512$ tokens) was identical between SFT (\textbf{15 / 26, 57.69\%}) and GRPO (\textbf{15 / 26, 57.69\%}). Accordingly, we classify this pilot GRPO result strictly as an \textbf{uninformative null because the policy did not meaningfully move}, rather than evidence of stability or reasoning gain; Q105 is not attributed to RL. Two structural factors explain this immobility: (1) high baseline solve rates ($92.0\%$) yielded zero advantage variance ($\sigma_r = 0$) on 10 / 25 prompts (only 15 active gradient steps), and (2) the LoRA adapter was trained at scale $s=1.0$ (\texttt{PeftModel}) but evaluated inside \texttt{UntrainedSoftMoAModel} at $s_{D1} = 0.25(1-\alpha) \approx 0.242$, attenuating the weight update by $\sim 4\times$ at inference time.

\subsection{Continuous Manifold Stabilization via Prefill Pondering ODE}

Integrating 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) relaxation ($\frac{dh}{dt} = \tanh(W_2 \mathrm{GELU}(W_1 h + b_1) + b_2)$) at the final transformer layer during prompt prefill preserved \textbf{100 / 125 (80.00\%)} on the clean held-out set (123/150 overall, 0 gains, 0 drops). We report this as a \textbf{null result regarding accuracy}: because final-layer prefill activations do not mutate earlier-layer KV caches in a causal decoder, the ODE acts strictly as a neutral invariance operator.

\subsection{Test-Time Neurosymbolic Verification and Null Latent Probing under Greedy Decoding (Clean-125 Exploratory Dev Ablation)}

To test whether autonomous test-time verification can detect and repair derivation errors without ground-truth leakage, we evaluated two progressive Process Reward Model (PRM) architectures on top of the 1536-token GRPO + Prefill Pondering ODE pipeline (Table~\ref{tab:step3_unified}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Module 2 --- CST Symbolic PRM \& Contradiction Gate (\texttt{eval\_grpo\_long\_cst\_prm\_150.py}):} Extracts intermediate equations ($L=R$) from the primary greedy chain (Pass 1) and checks algebraic consistency via SymPy alongside a Reasoning Chain Jacobian Filter ($\text{jump\_threshold}=3.5$). \subsection{Breaking Parity: Lens-Free Neurosymbolic Scoring and Selective Prediction}

\paragraph{Null / Underpowered Latent Probing Evidence (Tuned Lens Discarded):}
Across 125 exploratory dev greedy chains (only $n=22$ failed items, yielding $\approx \pm 0.13$ AUROC confidence width), Tuned Lens inter-layer stability across workspace layers ($28, 30 \to 31$) produced an uninformative, underpowered null signal:
\begin{equation}
\text{AUROC}(S_{\mathrm{mean}}) = 0.3721, \quad \text{AUROC}(S_{\mathrm{ans}}) = 0.4881, \quad \text{AUROC}(\text{Top-1}) = 0.4953.
\end{equation}
Rather than over-interpreting this small-$n$ null as a general theorem, we conclude empirically that Tuned Lens inter-layer stability does not separate passing from failing greedy derivations and harms candidate selection when weighted in the PRM; we therefore discard it in favor of calibrated token-probability / next-token Shannon entropy signals validated below.

\paragraph{Lens-Free Neurosymbolic Scoring Formulation:}
Eliminating the uninformative Tuned Lens term and coupling Semantic Agreement with upper-bounded CST symbolic equation verification yields the \textbf{Lens-Free Neurosymbolic PRM}:
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{LensFree}}(c) = 10 \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{has\_end}] + 8 \cdot \text{agree\_count}(c) + 1.5 \cdot \min(\text{eq\_count}(c), 10) - 8 \cdot |\text{contras}(c)| - 2 \cdot \text{wait\_cnt}(c),
\end{equation}
where $\text{agree\_count}(c) \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ denotes the semantic equivalence cluster cardinality among the $G=4$ candidates verified via \texttt{NormalizedMathMatcher}, and $\min(\text{eq\_count}, 10)$ enforces an equation-capping ceiling that prevents adversarial reward hacking via repetitive trivial derivations.

As documented in Table~\ref{tab:clean125_selective_ablation}, this scoring rule decisively breaks the PRM-Majority Voting parity: on Q124, candidate agreement elevates the true answer ($8$, agreement $2/4$) above the divergent branch ($12$, agreement $1/4$), while on Q138, the CST contradiction penalty retains $204_5$ ($0$ contras) over $224_5$ ($2$ contras). Consequently, the Lens-Free PRM achieves \textbf{107 / 125 (85.60\%)} with \textbf{zero regressions} ($+4$ net over SFT Baseline, $+3$ net over Module 2 Greedy Primary, McNemar $p = 0.1250$), simultaneously capturing both the consensus-driven and symbolic-filtered recovery modes.

\paragraph{Ternary Reward Simulation and the Tier-1 Selective Abstention Gate:}
To formalize the capacity to say ``I do not know'' rather than deliver confident hallucinations, we modeled an out-of-fold ternary decision policy:
\begin{equation}
\text{Utility} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \begin{cases} +1.0, & \text{if answered and correct} \\ 0.0, & \text{if abstained} \\ -\lambda, & \text{if answered and incorrect} \end{cases}
\end{equation}
Under 5-fold clustered cross-validation across penalty weights $\lambda \in [0.25, 5.00]$, calibrated decision thresholds $\tau^*(\lambda)$ prevent total abstention collapse ($\text{Coverage} \ge 42.0\%$ preserved even at $\lambda = 5.00$). At standard symmetric penalty $\lambda = 1.00$ (Bayes threshold $P^* = 0.50$), out-of-fold utility improves from $+0.1250$ (unselective forced answering) to \textbf{$+0.3950$} ($+0.2700$ net gain) at $68.0\%$ coverage and $78.9\%$ accuracy.

On the Clean-125 Held-Out benchmark, applying this principle yields the \textbf{Tier-1 Selective Abstention Gate}: when all four generated candidates completely diverge ($1/4$ agreement: Q027, Q044, Q097, Q101, Q127), the system emits an explicit abstention token. Because the model erred on 100\% (5/5) of these fully divergent cases, this gate prunes five confirmed hallucinations with \textbf{zero false abstentions (zero correct answers discarded)}, elevating Clean-125 accuracy from $82.40\%$ to \textbf{89.17\%} at $96.0\%$ coverage ($107 / 120$ answered problems correct, reducing total errors from 22 to 13, and increasing net utility from $+0.6480$ to $+0.7520$).

When evaluated on the strictly unseen Pristine-20 cohort (Q151..Q170), the same Tier-1 gate triggered exclusively on Q169 (token-limit exhaustion at 2048 tokens), pruning this confirmed hallucination without discarding any correct answers and elevating unseen accuracy from $70.00\%$ ($14/20$, after forensic resolution of a decimal extraction bug on Q160) to \textbf{73.68\%} ($14/19$) at $95.0\%$ coverage. Across the combined Clean-145 held-out pool (Q026..Q170), the Six-Arkhe Lens-Free pipeline achieves \textbf{87.05\%} selective accuracy ($121 / 139$) at $95.86\%$ coverage, pruning 6 out of 6 hallucinations ($100\%$ true abstention precision) with zero false abstentions.

By contrast, a PCA(16) logistic probe on late final-token hidden states (\texttt{probe\_h\_late\_tok}) failed paired bootstrap superiority over mean token logprob ($\Delta\text{AUROC}=0.1417$, $95\%\text{ CI: }[-0.0097, 0.2992]$, $p=0.0696$, dropping to $0.6484$ OOD; mean-pooled \texttt{probe\_h\_late\_mean} attained $0.7339$ CV and $0.9011$ on the small $N=20$ OOD cohort without beating entropy in-distribution), establishing entropy/logprob signals as the empirically reliable calibration backbone alongside negligible parameter Frobenius drift ($0.2891\%$ global, $0.9258\%$ max layer). Because cross-interpreter upgrades (\texttt{Python 3.10/PyTorch 2.5} $\to$ \texttt{Python 3.13/PyTorch 2.8}) induce numerical greedy trajectory shifts ($24/30 = 80.0\%$ token-identical, $30/30$ verdict-identical in \texttt{ENV\_EQUIVALENCE\_REPORT\_PY310\_VS\_PY313.json}), strict Epoch I lock protocols were executed: the same-stack \texttt{Python 3.13.7} SFT baseline re-capture across \texttt{Q026..Q200} completed at $\mathbf{139 / 175}$ ($\mathbf{79.43\%}$; note that Table~\ref{tab:pristine50_always_on} treats \texttt{Clean-125} and \texttt{Pristine-50} strictly as exploratory/pre-confirmatory cohorts), an interim local \texttt{Independent-400} audit on 96/100 MATH-train prompts ($384/400$ rollouts, \texttt{symbolic\_checker\_independent\_400\_results.json}) measured out-of-sample \texttt{SymbolicStepChecker} precision $\mathbf{68.29\%}$ ($\text{TP}=28, \text{FP}=13$), recall $\mathbf{15.73\%}$ ($\text{FN}=150$), and $\text{FPR}=6.31\%$ (full $400/400$ run + disjoint \texttt{Dev-500} active on Kaggle T4~$\times$~2), while movement-conditioned GRPO-1/GRPO-2 and strictly sealed \texttt{Q201..Q500} confirmatory testing remain \texttt{[PENDING]} under \texttt{Q201\_Q500\_CONFIRMATORY\_ANALYSIS\_PLAN.md}.

\section{Related Work}

Self-correction in LLMs~\cite{madaan2023selfrefine,shinn2023reflexion} requires ground-truth verifiers to avoid accuracy degradation~\cite{huang2023cannot}, which we corroborate at the latent level via the Tuned Lens~\cite{belrose2023tunedlens}. Preference optimization via DPO~\cite{rafailov2023dpo}, STaR~\cite{zelikman2022star}, and critic-free GRPO~\cite{shao2024deepseekmath} enables reasoning alignment, which we adapt to 8GB GPUs alongside test-time budget forcing~\cite{muennighoff2025s1}. Parameter-efficient adaptation via LoRA~\cite{hu2021lora} and QLoRA~\cite{dettmers2023qlora} faces cross-task interference in multi-adapter composition~\cite{huang2023lorahub}, which we resolve via inference-time soft blending. Continuous latent trajectories build upon Neural ODEs~\cite{chen2018neuralode} and Gated Delta Networks~\cite{yang2024gateddelta}.

\section{Discussion, Limitations \& Conclusion}

Our investigation highlights several methodological boundaries: (1) sample sizes ($N=35$ failure cohort with $p=0.114$, and $N=125$ clean held-out GRPO cohort with $p=1.0000$) are underpowered for asymptotic significance, and 4/35 falls within baseline parameter perturbation churn (9.3\%--21.4\%); (2) high baseline pass rates on the 25 RL prompts caused zero intra-group advantage variance ($\sigma_r=0$) on most steps, requiring larger difficulty-stratified pools ($N \ge 1000$, $0.2 \le p \le 0.8$ and the locked disjoint \texttt{Dev-500} pool) in ongoing Epoch I work; and (3) both Pondering ODE (Module 1) and deterministic single-retry PRM gating (Modules 2 \& 3) yielded zero net new solved problems over GRPO Long on Clean 125, as failed greedy chains exhibit high inter-layer stability ($0.7867$ vs.\ $0.7756$), whereas next-token Shannon entropy (\texttt{neg\_mean\_entropy}: $0.7583$ on silent failures, $0.8791$ OOD) and Pass-1 \texttt{min\_step\_logprob} ($\text{AUROC}=0.9510$ on Pristine-50) provide robust single-pass calibration. By establishing placebo negative controls, symmetrically rescoring the 500-problem baseline ($398/500$), calibrating \texttt{SymbolicStepChecker} precision to $72.7\%$, validating unseen Pristine-50 selective abstention ($90.91\%$ at $88\%$ coverage / $83.67\%$ at $98\%$ coverage), and locking cross-interpreter runtime determinism, this work provides a reproducible foundation for resource-efficient reasoning research.
