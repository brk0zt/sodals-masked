\begin{abstract}
Deploying 9B-parameter State Space Models (SSMs) on edge cyber-physical systems (CPS) introduces severe systems engineering challenges: non-deterministic garbage collection (GC) latency spikes, excessive memory footprints, numerical divergence in quantized recurrent state updates, and state desynchronization during backtracking search. We present \textbf{EdgeSSM}, a deterministic, zero-heap-allocation inference engine written in bare-metal Rust and CUDA for industrial telemetry diagnostics and predictive maintenance. EdgeSSM introduces five core systems innovations: (1) a stack-allocated, lock-free runtime (\texttt{RtRingBuffer}) that eliminates dynamic heap allocations during the token generation hot loop, confining application orchestrator memory to a strict 100~MB RAM ceiling alongside 5.5~GB GPU VRAM; (2) the identification and resolution of a critical GGUF head-expansion memory layout bug (\texttt{tile} vs.\ \texttt{repeat\_interleave}) in selective DeltaNet SSM kernels, elevating Layer-0 cosine similarity from 0.0300 to 0.9996 and reducing Layer-0 softmax projection Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence from 7.22 to $3.83 \times 10^{-8}$ (while maintaining a global cosine similarity of 0.9655 at the final Layer-32 logits across full autoregressive rollouts); (3) a CUDA-accelerated Compressed Sparse Row (CSR) Graph Neural Network (GNN) attention bias injector with $O(N \log \text{deg}_{\max})$ per-query row complexity (where $\text{deg}_{\max} \ll N$ is the maximum equipment node degree, decoupled from head dimension $d_k=128$) and thread-local accumulation; (4) a conflict-directed backtracking and cache synchronization protocol with nogood recording that resets recurrent SSM states in 0.22~ms of host orchestration time followed by a parallel GPU prefix re-prefill ($12.7\text{--}46.2\text{ ms}$ total end-to-end rollback latency, triggered in 7.5\% of diagnostic cases); and (5) continuous inference-time Soft LoRA Blending governed by a Layer-32 Gaussian Phase Router (GPR) with 1.3~ms routing overhead. Evaluated across 223 industrial diagnostic scenarios---comprising an AssetOpsBench-Derived Diagnostic Subset ($N=120$), Site Reliability Engineering (SRE, $N=38$), FinOps ($N=15$), and EdgeTelemetry-50 ($N=50$)---EdgeSSM achieves 80.0\% diagnostic exact-match accuracy (+21.7 percentage points over the unsteered local baseline, Fisher exact $p=0.0004$) and matches commercial cloud frontier models (GPT-4o, 78.3\%, Fisher exact $p=0.87$) while operating completely offline at 28.5~ms/token decode latency.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\IEEEPARstart{M}{odern} industrial cyber-physical systems (CPS), supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) networks, and smart manufacturing plants generate high-velocity multi-sensor telemetry streams~\cite{lee2015cps,iso13374}. Automating root-cause analysis (RCA), fault isolation, and maintenance triage in these environments requires models capable of synthesizing unstructured operator logs, physical equipment topologies, and continuous vibration/thermal telemetry. While cloud-hosted frontier Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT-4o exhibit strong diagnostic reasoning, deploying them in safety-critical industrial facilities is frequently precluded by three operational barriers: (1) strict data sovereignty and air-gapped network isolation mandates, (2) unpredictable wide-area network round-trip latencies (120--500~ms), and (3) lack of deterministic safety interlocks against hallucinated maintenance actions.

Transitioning 9B-parameter foundation models to on-premise industrial edge hardware (e.g., embedded workstations equipped with 8~GB consumer/industrial GPUs) via selective State Space Models (SSMs)~\cite{gu2023mamba,yang2024gateddelta} offers linear $O(L)$ sequence scaling and constant $O(1)$ per-step recurrent memory. However, standard Python/PyTorch inference stacks suffer from garbage collection (GC) pauses, unbounded heap fragmentation, and dynamic memory allocation spikes that violate real-time supervisory predictability~\cite{kopetz2011realtime}. Furthermore, combining recurrent SSM layers with standard Transformer attention layers introduces three subtle systems failure modes on quantized edge hardware:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{GGUF Head-Expansion Layout Divergence:} Naive tensor head expansion (\texttt{repeat\_interleave}) scrambles key-query group alignments against tiled GGUF quantization blocks~\cite{gerganov2023llamacpp}, causing immediate numerical collapse at Layer 0.
    \item \textbf{Recurrent State Desynchronization on Rollback:} Unlike standard Transformer KV caches that can be rewound via pointer truncation, the compressed recurrent state $\mathbf{S}_t$ in selective DeltaNet layers is updated via an affine rank-1 transformation. While algebraically invertible in exact arithmetic whenever $(1 - \beta_t \|\mathbf{k}_t\|^2) \neq 0$, backward inversion in finite floating-point precision (FP16/BF16) is severely ill-conditioned, causing exponential round-off explosion during constraint backtracking.
    \item \textbf{Cross-Subspace Adapter Interference:} Jointly fine-tuning multiple low-rank adapters (LoRA)~\cite{hu2022lora} across distinct plant competencies induces cross-subspace covariance perturbations ($\mathbf{A}_1 \mathbf{A}_2^T \neq 0$), degrading specialized diagnostic invariants.
\end{enumerate}

To address these challenges, we present \textbf{EdgeSSM}, a bare-metal Rust and CUDA inference engine engineered for deterministic edge CPS diagnostics. Our primary contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Bounded-Memory Deterministic Runtime:} A zero-heap-allocation Rust hot loop (\texttt{RtRingBuffer}) delivering predictable 28.5~ms per-token single-stream decode latency ($\sim 35\text{ tok/s}$) and 1.0--1.3~ms auxiliary routing cycles within a fixed 100~MB application orchestrator RAM budget and 5.5~GB GPU VRAM.
    \item \textbf{GGUF Head Expansion Correction:} Resolving the tiled head expansion mismatch in Qwen 3.5 SSM kernels, increasing Layer-0 cosine similarity from 0.0300 to 0.9996 and reducing Layer-0 softmax projection KL divergence from 7.22 to $3.83 \times 10^{-8}$ (retaining 0.9655 global cosine similarity at final Layer-32 logits under 4-bit Q4\_K\_M quantization).
    \item \textbf{DeltaNet Recurrent State Cache Synchronization:} A conflict-directed backtracking protocol with a nogood conflict cache that resets host cache pointers in 0.22~ms ($T_{\text{host}}$) and executes a parallel GPU chunk re-prefill ($T_{\text{GPU}} = 12.5\text{--}46.0\text{ ms}$, total rollback $12.7\text{--}46.2\text{ ms}$), guaranteeing exact state synchronization without storing 25.2~MB per-step state snapshots.
    \item \textbf{Thread-Local CUDA CSR Graph Attention \& Rule Gating:} Injecting physical plant connectivity via Compressed Sparse Row (CSR) binary search in $O(N \log \text{deg}_{\max})$ per-query row complexity without non-deterministic atomic reductions, coupled with EBNF grammar priors.
    \item \textbf{Empirical Competence on Industrial Telemetry Benchmarks:} Comprehensive evaluation across 223 cases (AssetOpsBench-Derived Diagnostic Subset $N=120$~\cite{patel2025assetops}, SRE $N=38$, FinOps $N=15$, EdgeTelemetry-50 $N=50$), demonstrating 80.0\% diagnostic exact-match accuracy and statistical parity with cloud-hosted GPT-4o (78.3\%, $p=0.87$).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}

\subsection{State Space Models \& Hybrid Sequence Architectures}
Selective State Space Models such as Mamba~\cite{gu2023mamba}, RWKV~\cite{peng2023rwkv}, S4~\cite{gu2021s4}, Hyena~\cite{poli2023hyena}, and Liquid Time-Constant Networks~\cite{hasani2021liquid} achieve linear $O(L)$ scaling in sequence length. Gated Delta Networks (DeltaNet)~\cite{yang2024gateddelta} refine recurrent memory compression using gated delta-rule updates complemented by IO-aware FlashAttention~\cite{dao2022flashattention}. EdgeSSM builds upon hybrid 3:1 DeltaNet-Attention architectures, resolving critical quantized tensor layout mismatches on edge GPUs.

\subsection{Edge AI \& Industrial Condition Monitoring}
Industrial predictive maintenance relies on multi-sensor anomaly detection~\cite{zhao2019dl,malhotra2016lstm} governed by condition monitoring standards such as ISO 13374-1~\cite{iso13374} and IEEE Std 1451.4~\cite{ieee1451}, within CPS frameworks~\cite{schwabacher2005prognostics,jardine2006cbm,lee2015cps}. Relational Graph Neural Networks (GCNs~\cite{kipf2017gcn}, GATs~\cite{velickovic2018gat}, GraphSAGE~\cite{hamilton2017graphsage}, R-GCNs~\cite{schlichtkrull2018rgcn}) capture topological sensor dependencies, while neuro-symbolic architectures~\cite{kautz2022thirdwave,trinh2024alphageometry} integrate formal rules. Real-time embedded systems require bounded execution without GC jitter~\cite{kopetz2011realtime}, which Rust enforces via compile-time ownership and zero-cost abstractions~\cite{klabnik2019rust}.

\subsection{Constrained Decoding, Backtracking \& Adapter Routing}
Conflict-directed backtracking and nogood recording~\cite{marquessilva1999grasp,biere2009sat} prune invalid search branches in automated reasoning, while grammar-constrained decoding (Outlines~\cite{willard2023outlines}, PICARD~\cite{scholak2021picard}) enforces syntactic validity. Parameter-efficient adaptation via LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora}, QLoRA~\cite{dettmers2023qlora}, and GPTQ~\cite{frantar2022gptq}, alongside intermediate layer probing via Tuned Lens~\cite{belrose2023tunedlens}, enables modular specialization. EdgeSSM unifies conflict-directed nogood caching with recurrent SSM prefix re-prefilling and continuous Layer-32 soft adapter blending.

\section{System Architecture \& Methodology}

EdgeSSM is implemented in native Rust (\texttt{sodals\_neuro}, \texttt{qwen\_custom.rs}) with custom CUDA FFI kernels, executing a 32-layer hybrid backbone (24 recurrent DeltaNet SSM layers and 8 full attention layers at indices 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31).

\subsection{Bounded-Allocation Rust Runtime (\texttt{RtRingBuffer})}
To eliminate dynamic heap allocations (\texttt{malloc}/\texttt{free}) and garbage collection pauses during autoregressive decoding, EdgeSSM pre-allocates a fixed 100~MB application orchestrator arena at initialization using Rust \texttt{const generics} and lock-free circular ring buffers (\texttt{RtRingBuffer<T, N>}). While total host process Resident Set Size (RSS) including the initialized CUDA driver context spans 600--900~MB, application-level orchestrator heap growth is strictly zero ($0\text{ B}$ allocated during the token generation hot loop).

\subsection{Selective SSM Core \& GGUF Head-Expansion Alignment}
Within each recurrent DeltaNet layer, the state matrix $\mathbf{S}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{state}} \times d_{\text{head}}}$ ($d_{\text{state}} = 128$) updates via the gated delta rule:
\begin{align}
\mathbf{g}_t &= \mathrm{softplus}(\mathbf{x}_t) \odot \mathbf{a}_{\text{ssm}}, \quad \mathbf{S}_{\text{dec}} = \mathbf{S}_{t-1} \odot \exp(\mathbf{g}_t) \\
\mathbf{e}_t &= \mathbf{v}_t - \mathbf{k}_t^T \mathbf{S}_{\text{dec}}, \quad \mathbf{\Delta}_t = \mathbf{e}_t \odot \mathbf{\beta}_t \\
\mathbf{S}_t &= \mathbf{S}_{\text{dec}} + \mathbf{k}_t \otimes \mathbf{\Delta}_t, \quad \mathbf{o}_t = \frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{\text{state}}}} \mathbf{q}_t \cdot \mathbf{S}_t
\end{align}

In Qwen 3.5 SSM layers, 16 key/query head groups expand to 32 groups. Reference GGUF weights~\cite{gerganov2023llamacpp} serialize head groups in tiled order:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{k}_{\text{tiled}} = [h_0, h_1, \dots, h_{15}, h_0, h_1, \dots, h_{15}]
\end{equation}
whereas naive \texttt{repeat\_interleave(2, dim=head)} produces interleaved pairs $[h_0, h_0, \dots, h_{15}, h_{15}]$, scrambling projections across all 24 SSM layers (collapsing Layer-0 cosine similarity to $0.0300$ and surging KL divergence to $7.22$). EdgeSSM enforces explicit tiled concatenation $\mathrm{Concat}(\mathbf{k}_{\text{heads}}, \mathbf{k}_{\text{heads}}, \text{dim}=\text{head})$, restoring Layer-0 cosine similarity to \textbf{0.9996} and Layer-0 softmax projection KL divergence to $\mathbf{3.83 \times 10^{-8}}$.

\subsection{Deterministic EBNF Rule Priors \& CUDA CSR Graph Attention}
Industrial operating envelopes are compiled into Extended Backus-Naur Form (EBNF) domain cartridges. Candidate tokens matching valid safety rules receive a deterministic checksum prior:
\begin{equation}
\text{bias}_{\text{rule}} = \left( \sum_{b \in \mathrm{bytes}(R)} b \pmod{1024} \right) \times 10^{-4} \in [0, 0.1024)
\end{equation}
acting strictly as a reproducible tie-breaker among valid tokens without distorting model probabilities.

Physical plant topology is encoded as a Compressed Sparse Row (CSR) adjacency matrix $\mathbf{A}_{\text{csr}} = (\text{indptr}, \text{indices}, \text{data})$. During Transformer attention layers, our custom CUDA kernel \texttt{apply\_gnn\_bias\_kernel} maps each thread to a unique query-key coordinate $(q_{\text{idx}}, k_{\text{idx}})$ and executes a binary search over the CSR edge list:
\begin{lstlisting}[language=C++]
size_t row_start = indptr[q_nodes[q_idx]];
size_t row_end   = indptr[q_nodes[q_idx] + 1];
int low = row_start, high = row_end - 1;
while (low <= high) {
    int mid = low + (high - low) / 2;
    if (indices[mid] == k_nodes[k_idx]) {
        scores[idx] += gnn_weight * data[mid];
        break;
    } else if (indices[mid] < k_nodes[k_idx]) low = mid + 1;
    else high = mid - 1;
}
\end{lstlisting}
This bounds relational lookup complexity to $O(N \log \text{deg}_{\max})$ per query row (total $O(N^2 \log \text{deg}_{\max})$ across sequence length $N$, where $\text{deg}_{\max} \ll N$ is the maximum equipment node degree, completely decoupled from head dimension $d_k=128$) and uses direct thread-local writes rather than non-deterministic \texttt{atomicAdd} reductions.

\subsection{Five-Phase Stabilization \& Conflict-Directed Backtracking}
To prevent reasoning collapse under 4-bit quantization, EdgeSSM enforces a five-phase runtime protocol: (1) a minimum thinking token floor ($M=1024$) suppressing premature \texttt{</think>} emission; (2) dynamic post-softmax \texttt{min\_p} filtering ($\text{min\_p}=0.05$); (3) structural-token-safe Skew-G repetition braking ($\text{logits} \leftarrow \text{logits} \cdot 0.85$ on cyclic high-skew tokens while exempting structural delimiters); (4) Tuned Lens~\cite{belrose2023tunedlens} observational entropy monitoring; and (5) conflict-directed backtracking.

\paragraph{Numerical Conditioning \& Cache Synchronization:}
The delta-rule update is an affine transformation:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{S}_t = \mathbf{S}_{t-1} \mathrm{diag}(\exp(\mathbf{g}_t)) (\mathbf{I} - \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{k}_t^T) + \beta_t \mathbf{v}_t \mathbf{k}_t^T
\end{equation}
While formally invertible in exact arithmetic via the Sherman-Morrison formula whenever $(1 - \beta_t \|\mathbf{k}_t\|^2) \neq 0$, backward inversion in FP16/BF16 is severely ill-conditioned, amplifying round-off errors exponentially. Furthermore, saving explicit state checkpoints across 24 SSM layers requires $25.2\text{ MB}$ per node. Instead, when a diagnostic path violates a safety invariant, EdgeSSM records the conflict pattern in a hash-indexed \textbf{Nogood Cache} (\texttt{NogoodClauseCache}), truncates the token history to $L_{\text{prefix}}$, resets both attention KV and SSM caches in \textbf{0.22~ms} of host CPU orchestration time ($T_{\text{host}}$), and executes a single parallel GPU chunk re-prefill over the retained prefix ($L \le 512$), requiring \textbf{12.5--46.0~ms} on the GPU ($12.7\text{--}46.2\text{ ms}$ total end-to-end rollback latency).

\subsection{Modular Cartridge Isolation \& Soft LoRA Blending}
To prevent the Subspace Interference Effect ($\mathbf{A}_1 \mathbf{A}_2^T \neq 0$) observed during joint multi-adapter training~\cite{ozturk2026sodals}, domain cartridges are trained in strict isolation and blended continuously at inference time via a Layer-32 Gaussian Phase Router ($\tau = 0.004913$, Fisher separation $= 15.30$):
\begin{equation}
\Delta \mathbf{W} = (1 - \alpha) s_1 \mathbf{W}_1 + \alpha s_2 \mathbf{W}_2, \quad \alpha = \sigma(z_i / 0.03)
\end{equation}
Synthesized distillation trajectories are audited via Wronskian SVD residual norms ($\text{residual}(v) > 0.30$) and Jacobian step-consistency filters prior to offline QLoRA training~\cite{dettmers2023qlora,frantar2022gptq}.

All evaluations were executed on the native bounded-allocation Rust runtime on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU (8~GB VRAM, Ada Lovelace) and Intel Core i9-13900HX CPU (64~GB DDR5 RAM). Binomial accuracies are reported with \textbf{Wilson Score 95\% Confidence Intervals}~\cite{wilson1927ci}.

    \item \textbf{SRE Troubleshooting Suite ($N = 38$)}: Microservice and edge topology root-cause isolation scenarios.
    \item \textbf{FinOps Scenario Suite ($N = 15$)}: Compute and power budget anomaly detection under hardware constraints.

\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion \& Methodological Boundaries}

EdgeSSM operates within documented boundaries: (1) with a 1,024-token reasoning floor at 28.5~ms/token ($\sim 29\text{ s}$ total response time), EdgeSSM serves as a SCADA-level supervisory diagnostic advisor rather than a sub-millisecond PLC actuator loop; (2) unsteered baselines were evaluated with zero-shot natural language prompts without in-context EBNF rule injection; (3) statistical parity with GPT-4o reflects overlapping Wilson CIs ($p=0.87$) rather than formal Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) equivalence; (4) single-stream decoding (35~tok/s) prioritizes zero-heap determinism over batched throughput; and (5) 103 of 223 scenarios were internally authored. Foundational analyses of self-distillation policy optimization and multi-adapter subspace interference on formal mathematical benchmarks are presented in our companion work~\cite{ozturk2026sodals}. As an engineering roadmap following Python \texttt{Dev-500} validation, we plan to port complex holomorphic phase rotation and 2-hop Sheaf-GNN diffusion operators serialized via \texttt{.qmem} directly into Rust (\texttt{qwen\_ssm.rs}, \texttt{qwen\_custom.rs}) with layer-level logit equivalence checks.

\section{Conclusion}

We presented EdgeSSM, a deterministic Rust/CUDA State Space Model inference engine for industrial cyber-physical systems. By combining GGUF \texttt{tile} layout alignment, $O(N \log \text{deg}_{\max})$ thread-local CSR graph attention, EBNF rule priors, conflict-directed nogood backtracking with prefix re-prefill, and continuous Soft LoRA Blending within a 100~MB orchestrator RAM budget and 5.5~GB GPU VRAM, EdgeSSM achieves 80.0\% diagnostic accuracy on AssetOpsBench ($+21.7\%$ over unsteered baseline), delivering cloud-comparable diagnostics completely offline on plant-floor edge hardware.
